# Privacy Policy
Last updated: 27 November 2026

## 1. Introduction
GlowUp ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application. Please read this privacy policy carefully. If you do not agree with the terms of this privacy policy, please do not access the application.

## 2. Information We Collect
We collect information that you voluntarily provide to us when registering for an account and while using the application. This includes:

- Personal Identifiers: your first name, middle name, last name, date of birth, age, and email address.
- Profile and Preference Data: your skin type, skin tone, optional skin reactions, and known cosmetic allergens and sensitivities. This information is used exclusively to tailor the app's safety analyses and product recommendations to your personal profile.
- Scan Data: the ingredient text read from product labels, the results of each analysis, the products you save to your Recent Scans, and any names you give them.
- Suggestions: allergens, or ingredients you add that are not yet in our lists, so that we can review them and improve the database.
- Technical Data: our cloud provider may process basic technical information, such as your IP address and device identifiers, in order to deliver and secure the service.

## 3. How We Use Your Information
Having accurate information permits us to provide you with a smooth, efficient, and customized experience. Specifically, we use information collected via GlowUp to:

- Create and secure your user account using Google Firebase Authentication.
- Confirm that the email address is yours. When you sign up with an email and password, your account is created as soon as the first sign-up page is filled in, and Google Firebase Authentication sends a message with a link to that address. The sign-up goes on once you have tapped the link. If you leave the sign-up before finishing it, that unfinished account is removed again.
- Make sure the email address you sign up with can really receive email. For an address that is not from a well-known provider, the app asks a public internet directory (DNS, run by Google or Cloudflare) whether the part after the @ (for example "school.edu.ph") has a mail server. Only that part is sent, never your name or the whole address. Temporary ("throw-away") email addresses cannot be used to sign up.
- Store and retrieve your profile preferences securely using Google Cloud Firestore.
- Personalize cosmetic ingredient safety assessments and filter recommendations based on your registered skin type, skin reactions, age, and allergies.
- Keep your scan history available across sessions and, when you are online, across your devices.
- Count how often a brand is scanned, without attaching your name to that count, so that we can prioritise which products to add to the database.
- Communicate with you regarding account updates, security alerts, and support messages.

## 4. Camera, Scans, and AR Try-On
GlowUp asks for permission to use your camera for two features: scanning a product and its ingredient list, and previewing shades on your face with AR Try-On.

- The text on a product is read on your device. We do not record or store the live camera feed. We keep a photo only when you choose to send one for a product that is not in our catalog yet (see "Product Photo Submissions" below).
- For a scan, only the text that was read, the product's name and the result are kept and analysed. When you are online and the app cannot name the product by itself, a few still pictures of the PRODUCT you are holding (its front, back or bottom; four at most, once per scan) may be sent to our AI service provider so that the product can be recognised (see section 5). Those pictures are not saved by us.
- So that the app knows a product again the next time you scan it, it keeps what its pack looks like as a set of numbers (not a picture; the picture cannot be rebuilt from them), together with any other name you typed for the product. These stay on your device only and are removed when you clear the app's data or uninstall the app.
- For AR Try-On, the picture of your face is used only while the preview is on screen. It is not saved and it is never sent to us or to anyone else.

You can withdraw camera permission at any time in your device settings. The scanning and try-on features will stop working until permission is given again, but you can still type ingredients in by hand.

## Product Photo Submissions
When a product you scan is not in our catalog yet, you can choose to take one photo of it and send it to us. Nothing is sent unless you tap "Use photo", and the app tells you before your first photo is sent that GlowUp will review it.

- What is stored: the photo, cut on your device to the part that shows the product (the rest of the picture is discarded before anything is sent); the text the app read on the product; the product's brand, name, type and shade; the ingredient list and the web address of the page it came from, or the list printed on the pack; the time you sent it; and your account id.
- Who sees it: GlowUp administrators, who review it in our admin portal. You can see your own photo; other app users cannot.
- Why: to identify the product, to find its published ingredient list, and to add the product to our catalog after an administrator has checked it. Until then it is shown to you as "Unverified — pending review".
- How it is processed: the cut photo and the text read on it are sent to our AI service provider only to recognise which product it is. The ingredient list is never written by the AI: it is taken from product pages our server opens (the brand's own website first, then retailers) or from the list printed on your pack. Ingredient data from the web may be incomplete.
- How long it is kept: the photo is deleted when an administrator approves or rejects the entry. The product details stay in our catalog once approved; they carry no information about you. If you want your photo or entry removed earlier, contact us (section 12).
- Where it is kept: in our Google Firebase database, protected by security rules that let only you and GlowUp administrators read the photo.

## 5. AI-Assisted Analysis
Part of each analysis is produced automatically. When you are online, the following may be sent to our AI service provider: up to four still pictures of the product, the text read from it, the product's name, and ingredient names. This is used to recognise the product, to find its published ingredient list, and to describe ingredients that are not yet in our database. The product's name may also be sent to the FDA Philippines verification site to check whether the product is registered. When the camera read the product's barcode, that barcode (and nothing else) may be sent to Open Beauty Facts, a public list of cosmetics, to compare its ingredient list with the one the app has.

Your profile is never sent to the AI service. Your name, email address, password, age, skin type, skin tone, skin reactions, and allergens stay out of every such request: the comparison between a product and your profile is made on your device.

Automated results can be incomplete or wrong. Please read section 4 of the Terms and Conditions below.

## 6. Data Security and Third-Party Services
We implement industry-standard security measures, including encrypted transit layers (HTTPS) and salted password hashing, to protect your personal information. Your password is never stored in readable form. We utilize Google Firebase as our primary cloud backend infrastructure. Google Firebase adheres to rigorous security standards. We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your personally identifiable information to outside parties.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security, and you use the application with that understanding.

## 7. Offline Use
To let GlowUp work without an internet connection, a copy of your profile, your scan history, and the ingredient database is kept on your device. Changes you make offline are sent to the cloud the next time you are online. If you share your device with other people, sign out when you are finished.

The email addresses that have signed in with a password on this device are also kept on the device, so that the log-in page can offer them under the email field. You can take an address off that list there at any time, and it is removed when you delete your account or clear the app's data in your phone's settings. Only the address is kept: passwords are never shown, offered, or stored in readable form. The app shows this list of its own instead of your phone's saved accounts.

## 8. Data Retention and Account Deletion
We retain your personal data only for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide you with app services. You can delete your account yourself at any time: open Settings and choose Delete My Account (you need an internet connection and your password). This removes your profile, your scan history and your sign-in from our database, and everything about the account on your phone. You can also ask our support team to delete it.

A few things are kept apart from your profile and are not removed by Delete My Account: the anonymous scan counts (they carry no name, email or account id), and the allergens, ingredients or product photos you sent us as suggestions, which carry your account id until our team has reviewed them. Ask our support team if you want those removed too.

## 9. Children's Privacy
GlowUp is not intended for children under 13 years of age, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, please contact us so that we can remove it.

## 10. Your Rights and Choices
GlowUp follows the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173) of the Philippines. Under that law you have the right to be informed about how your personal data is used, to see the data we hold about you, to correct it, to object to its use, to ask for it to be deleted or blocked, to receive a copy of it, to be compensated for damage caused by misuse of it, and to withdraw your consent at any time. You can update your profile in the app, delete your account in Settings, and contact us for anything else. If you think your data was handled wrongly, you may also complain to the National Privacy Commission (privacy.gov.ph).

Your skin type, skin tone, skin reactions and allergens are information about your health. The law treats such information as sensitive personal information, so we use it only with your clear consent. You give that consent with the second box on the page where you accept this policy, and you can withdraw it by deleting your account. Without that consent the app cannot compare products with your profile.

Your data is stored with Google Firebase (Google Cloud), whose servers are outside the Philippines. Google keeps it under its own security and data protection terms.

If a security breach puts your personal data at risk of serious harm, we will tell you and the National Privacy Commission within 72 hours of knowing about it, and say what happened and what you can do.

## 11. Changes to This Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the "Last updated" date at the top and, where the change is significant, tell you inside the app.

## 12. Contact Us
You can send a report from the app: Settings > Contact support. The report goes to the GlowUp team through our support dashboard, not by email. It contains the kind of report you chose (Bug, Wrong scan result or Other), your message, your account id, the app version and your phone model. Your last scan (the product, the result shown and the ingredients read) is included only when you switch on "Include my last scan". Only GlowUp administrators can read reports.

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, you can also contact our support team at: GlowUpAdminSupport@gmail.com
