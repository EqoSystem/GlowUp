FAQ

Good questions

Is this medical advice?

No. GlowUp gives information only. For a skin condition or a reaction, talk to a dermatologist.

What happens to my data?

Your skin profile is saved to your own account, and the comparison with a product happens on your phone. Your profile is never sent to the AI. The scan counts our team sees carry no name or account. You can delete your account and data in Settings.

Which phones does it work on?

Android phones. Shade try-on works best on phones with Google ARCore; others use a simpler face tracker.

Is it free?

Yes. GlowUp is a student research project and costs nothing to use.

What if a product isn't found?

Scan its label. The app reads the ingredients and sends the new product to our team to check and add.
