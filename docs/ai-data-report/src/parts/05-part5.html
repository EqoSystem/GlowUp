18. Verdict on belief B1

Partly true. The true part: for a product that is Active, clearly make-up, with a correct printed name, aliases and a barcode, the phone matches it from its own copy without the internet, and uses the admin's list at once. {V:Scripts/Data/ProductIndex.cs; Scripts/Data/ProductRepository.cs lines 61-83} What is not shown: "great results" and "quickly" have never been measured on a phone. {N:measured time to result and recognition rate on a phone} A correct definition also cannot help when the pack is unreadable, the product is not in the catalogue, or the entry is still Pending.

Safe panel wording: "The scan is only as good as the catalogue. When admins enter a product's printed name, other names, barcode and ingredient list correctly and set it Active, the phone can recognise it from its own copy, offline, without asking the AI. We have not yet measured the time on phones; that is our next test."
