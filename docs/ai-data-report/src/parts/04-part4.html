Finding. There is no switch, page or field called "Manual mode". {V:grep "manual" over _Admin_*, _Shared (only the scan source "typed in" on the Dashboard, _Admin_Dashboard/adminDashboard.js lines 327, 512)} The nearest behaviour is an admin writing the entry by hand instead of using Assist with AI. The website sends admins there in three cases: the AI found no source ("The AI found no source for this name. It may be a misread label or a trade name."), every AI provider refused ("Try again later, or write the entry by hand"), or the daily limit of AI assists in the browser was used ("Write this entry by hand, or go on tomorrow"). {V:api/assist-ingredient.js lines 688, 768; _Admin_Ingredient_Curator/adminIngredientCurator.js lines 662, 682, 695} A draft written by a backup AI without a web search is marked as such, names no sources, and all its boxes start unchecked. {V:_Admin_Ingredient_Curator/adminIngredientCurator.js line 689}

Belief B5 ("some brands/products have no credible sources, so admins must use Manual mode") is true in substance. Safe panel wording: "When Assist with AI finds no source page, the admin enters the data by hand from the packaging or the brand, and the entry stays Pending until it is reviewed." Do not call it "Manual mode" unless the team adds that name to the website.

15. Hand-entry checklist and verification status

Checklist for an admin writing an entry by hand

  1. Pack in hand (or a sharp photo of the back label); the market where it was bought.
  2. Brand chosen from the list (not typed again); product name exactly as printed.
  3. Cosmetic type from the 17 make-up types; the form refuses skin care. {V:_Admin_Brand_Inventory/adminBrandInventory.js lines 2318-2320}
  4. Ingredients in printed order, INCI names, one per comma.
  5. Shades as hex colours measured as in Section 24, not shade numbers.
  6. Barcode digits; FDA Philippines notification number if found.
  7. Source: the page title and https address, or "from packaging" with the date in the description.
  8. Status Pending until a second admin checks; then Active.

Verification status: what exists, and a proposal

Today an entry's trust is spread over status, reviewed, source and, for AI ingredients, webSearched. {V:FIRESTORE_SCHEMA.md lines 163-191} The app shows "PENDING APPROVAL" for snapshot entries, "Found by the AI lookup; not yet reviewed" for unreviewed AI ingredients, and where each list came from. {V:FIRESTORE_SCHEMA.md lines 125, 190; Scripts/Data/DataModels.cs line 179} There is no single verification field. {N:a verification status field on products and ingredients}
