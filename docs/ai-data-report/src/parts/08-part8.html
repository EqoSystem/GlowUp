Part 8 · Database rules26. How brand, product and ingredient data is stored

In plain words. One Firestore database serves the app and the website. Each brand, each product and each ingredient is one document. A product carries its own ingredient list as names (copied from the pack), not as links that must be followed, so the phone can analyse a product from one document plus its own ingredient table. {V:FIRESTORE_SCHEMA.md lines 5-18, 115-118}

ChoiceWhat and why
Product to ingredientEmbedded array of names (ingredients[]) plus a light summary of ids (ingredientIds[]). Why: no join and no mass update; the verdict is worked out at read time, so an ingredient's new warning applies to every product at once; a stored verdict would go stale. Per-product notes live on the product (ingredientNotes). {V:FIRESTORE_SCHEMA.md lines 54-56, 117-118}
Product to brandReference id brandId plus a copy of brandName; renaming a brand on the website updates the copies. {V:FIRESTORE_SCHEMA.md lines 45-46}
ShadesEmbedded shades[] of hex text on the product; no shade collection. {V:FIRESTORE_SCHEMA.md line 51}
Document idsWebsite entries: random Firestore ids. App entries: scan_<brand>__<name> made from the normalised names, so the same product scanned twice is one id; AI ingredients ai_; snapshots snap_<brand>__<name>__<uid>. {V:Scripts/Scan/ScannedProductPublisher.cs lines 19, 107; firestore.rules lines 58-82}
Search keystags[] (kind, brand, has-shades, no-ingredient-list, fda-listed; at most 20) for one-query filters on the website; normalised keys are computed in memory, not stored. {V:FIRESTORE_SCHEMA.md line 57; Scripts/Data/TextKey.cs}

What is too open, or missing

  • No rule says allow read, write: if true. Everything not listed is closed. {V:firestore.rules line 367}
  • Catalogue read is public, including Pending, Inactive and binned entries and the addedBy account id of app-added entries. The app needs to read every entry (to never add a product twice), so restricting it needs an app change first. {V:firestore.rules lines 130, 152, 215; Scripts/Data/ProductRepository.cs header "every product the database holds"}
  • Admin writes are only lightly checked: no status, category, cosmetic type or hex checks, no field allow-list. {V:firestore.rules lines 97-116}
  • App-added brands may say "Skincare". {V:firestore.rules line 143}
  • Any signed-in account (anonymous accounts too) can add scanActivity notes and read every pending_products entry (with the sender's account id). {V:firestore.rules lines 249-256, 285; FIRESTORE_SCHEMA.md line 142}
  • Admin check is a document read per request (exists(admins/uid)); custom claims are not used. {V:firestore.rules lines 19-21} {N:Firebase custom claims for admins}
  • Whether the file in the repository is the one published: last recorded publish 22 October 2026. {I:project notes; no deployment record in the repository} {N:a deployment record of the rules}

Proposed hardened ruleset: docs/ai-data-report/proposed.firestore.rules. It keeps every path as it is and tightens the catalogue: status and category values, make-up kinds by the app's own list of 17, hex shades, a field allow-list for admin writes, optional custom-claim admins, and no "Skincare" from the app. It is not deployed and must be tested on the Firestore emulator with the website's own flows before anyone publishes it.

30. Queries and performance

  • Phone: catalogue copies on the phone; entries changed since the last sync are fetched (updatedAt, createdAt, binnedAt, reviewedAt), every 10 minutes at most, with a whole download every 24 hours; reads are counted in the log. {V:Scripts/Data/CatalogSync.cs lines 16, 93, 146; Scripts/Scan/ScanRules.cs lines 34-36; Scripts/Data/ReadCounter.cs}
  • Scan: no Firestore query in the normal path (the phone index); one read by id for an own snapshot entry, a miss remembered 30 minutes. {V:FIRESTORE_SCHEMA.md line 146}
  • Website: lists kept in the browser, only changes downloaded; search, filters and export cost no reads. {V:FIRESTORE_SCHEMA.md line 119}
  • Pagination: the review window loads at most 50 waiting snapshots. {V:FIRESTORE_SCHEMA.md line 146}
