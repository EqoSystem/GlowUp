Part 11 · AR Try-On technical notes34. The AR try-on as it was found (audit)

In plain words. AR Try-On already used the real face mesh, not a flat sticker. On phones with Google's AR service it draws thin colour layers on ARCore's 468-point face mesh, so colour follows the face as it turns and the mouth opens. On other phones it falls back to a guided 2D preview over a face outline.

Face data and privacy

Camera frames, the face mesh and light samples live in memory only, per frame. Nothing in Scripts/AR/ encodes a picture or writes a file, and nothing is uploaded. {V:grep of Scripts/AR/ for EncodeToJPG, EncodeToPNG, WriteAllBytes, UnityWebRequest: no hit} The camera permission is asked after the screen is drawn; a refusal shows a card with a button to the phone's settings; no background camera use. {V:Scripts/AR/CameraWaitCard.cs; Scripts/Common/CameraPermission.cs}

Device coverage: live tracking needs an ARCore-supported phone with "Google Play Services for AR". Every other phone gets the guided preview. A MediaPipe face landmarker would widen coverage, but the project's MediaPipe plug-in (tasks-vision 0.10.11, used for finger signs and pack looks) was not wired for faces, and the project notes record it as not possible with the current plug-ins. {I:Scripts/Common/HandLandmarkReader.cs; Scripts/Scan/PackEmbedder.cs; developer notes of 21 October 2026}
