"Files": how admin documents become data

The Brand & Product Inventory and the Ingredient Curator accept PDF, Excel and Word files. Two paths:

  1. The GlowUp import format (blocks === PRODUCT === with KEY: value lines, lists with | ; Excel row 1 = keys): read as it is by _Shared/importFormat.js, no AI. {V:IMPORT_FORMAT.md}
  2. Any other document: its text is extracted in the browser (_Shared/adminDocumentReader.js) and only the text is sent to the admin-only function api/assist-ingredient.js, which returns records. Image-only PDFs and old .doc files are not supported. {V:FIRESTORE_SCHEMA.md line 65}

Either way: imported records arrive as Pending / not reviewed, never overwrite an existing product, skin care is flagged and not saved, and a shade NUMBER is never taken for a colour. Limits: 40 products / 60 ingredients per file. {V:FIRESTORE_SCHEMA.md line 67; IMPORT_FORMAT.md; _Admin_Brand_Inventory/adminBrandInventory.js lines 1032-1046}

Chronic skin condition (belief B6): verified

A product is suggested only if it is make-up, has an ingredient list, the user is old enough, and no ingredient is flagged. For a user who saved a skin condition, two more rules: the list must come from a source (an admin typed or reviewed it, or pages are on file), and every ingredient must be known (0 unknown allowed by default). Skin care is never suggested. A suggestion means "no ingredients flagged for your profile", never "safe" or "good for your condition". {V:Scripts/Scan/ProductRecommender.cs header} The make-up-only rule is written twice, word for word, in the app and on the website, and tested against the same example lists. {V:FIRESTORE_SCHEMA.md "Make-up only"; Scripts/Scan/ProductScope.cs; _Shared/glowupRules.js}

One gap: the database rules still accept a brand the app adds with category Skincare, and their make-up check for app-added products is looser than the app's. {V:firestore.rules lines 37-51, 143; FIRESTORE_SCHEMA.md line 251}
